2. Purpose and audience
VerifyRadar helps people in Brazil who buy cars at auction decide a maximum bid. It lists upcoming vehicle lots, compares the current bid with the FIPE reference price, and can produce an AI bid cap (risk, estimated resale, costs, and a suggested maximum bid). The free account includes the full catalog and 3 AI analyses per month. A paid subscription is optional.

4. External services
- Public vehicle-auction listings (Sodré Santoro and Palácio dos Leilões) and the Brazilian FIPE reference table, loaded by our server.
- Google Gemini, used only to write the AI bid-cap analysis.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Subscription status is confirmed with our server after purchase. There is no web, WhatsApp, or third-party checkout in the app.

5. Regions
The same features are available in every App Store region. The catalog content is Brazilian auction lots, and the interface is Portuguese (Brazil). Subscription price follows the local App Store price tier.

6. Regulated industry and third-party material
The app is not an auction house, lender, insurer, or broker, and it does not sell vehicles. Listings are publicly published by the auction houses; the lot screen links to the official auction page. FIPE figures and AI bid caps are informational estimates, not a purchase offer. No additional license credential is required to review the app.

7. In-App Purchases
Two auto-renewable monthly subscriptions, both with a 7-day introductory offer:
- radar_monthly — Radar — R$ 34,90/month — 20 AI analyses/month and alert filters.
- radar_pro_monthly — Radar Pro — R$ 49,90/month — 80 AI analyses/month, a more detailed report, and more alert filters.
Open the Planos tab (also shown after sign-up, with “Continuar grátis” to skip). Each plan shows the title, “Mensal”, the price, the 7-day intro, auto-renewal terms, Restore Purchases, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and the Apple Standard EULA.
Users who already paid on our website see an active plan and no buy button. The iOS app never links to an external purchase.
